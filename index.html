About

Macroeconomics, expectations, beliefs, and policy transmission.

I am pursuing a PhD in Economics at Indiana University Bloomington. Before Indiana, I studied econometrics and mathematical economics at the London School of Economics and economics at Nanjing University.

This site is a living home for my research, teaching, notes, and tools for following the U.S. economy and academic conferences.
